Peers are declared, so the SNI universe is closed at lane parse time
and the data structure should encode it: a lazy cache models an open
world the mediator's SNI gate already forbids (that gate is the only
issuer path). Discarding the CA private key before the constructor
returns shrinks the key-material window from the lane's duration to the
constructor call. The constructor-signature ripple was measured at the
proposing anchor: 18 transport.New call sites in 10 files, one of them
production. Whether a closed state survives as an atomic or Close
simplifies to leaf-key disposal is fixed at instruction time.
Independent of item-0136.
