Parked by operator decision of 2026-11-10 while deciding the publication
model (item-0174): a possible extension, not relevant before the beta, by
the operator's estimate an even chance of never being needed.

Two aspects motivate it.

- Completeness. The register line of ADR-057 D6 already records the bundle
  digests. Reading them by digest would let verify name a bundle that a
  registry omits or lists late, where today a required layer ends in FAIL
  after the bounded wait and a missing authorization bundle in the grade
  candidate. Neither case passes falsely today.
- Retraction. Under ADR-057 D6 a retracted digest keeps the grade release,
  because the tree, not the registry, says what is released, and verify
  does not read the tree. A consumer who relies on strike verify alone
  accepts a retracted digest.

The cost is an input channel for the register, a contract for a line that
is procedure prose today, and, for the retraction aspect, a revision of
ADR-057 D6 and D7. cosign and Beta DoD row 6 are unaffected.
