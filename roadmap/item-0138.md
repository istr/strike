The during-serve snapshot contract was load-bearing only through
accidental sequencing: the run path snapshots records at step end while
serve continues to lane end, and the deploy defers read records before
Stop. Both are sequencing swaps; a capsule's purpose ends when its
container is reaped, so stopping it at step end also removes listener
surface without function. A single collector fed by a channel preserves
arrival order, which matters because the records enter the attestation.
The mediator already builds its tls.Config per connection, so the
handshake-denial records can flow through a per-connection recorder
closure instead of a method on the shared mediator. Sequenced after
item-0136 (shared surfaces: mediator, capsule, deploy, run).
