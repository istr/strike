Post-beta. Found after item-0142, read at 62fecd5. Fuzzing has been required
by the docs since fb7d377 (2026-04-01), but the module has had only one fuzz
target, FuzzParseRef, added in d00b3bc and deleted with ParseRef in 232bece
(2026-06-15). lane.Parse and registry.SpecHash have been listed since the
start and never had a target; executor.validateContentType was listed and
removed in the next commit (23fed00). No testdata/fuzz corpus exists. The
two fuzz modules in go.sum are checksum lines only, pulled in by
sigstore/rekor and theupdateframework/go-tuf; strike imports neither, so
nothing on that side moves with this item.

Until the item lands, section 2.6 of docs/DEVELOPMENT.md and the word
"fuzz" in the AGENTS.md testing pointer describe a practice the repository
does not follow.
