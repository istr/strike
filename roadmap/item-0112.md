ADR-051 D7.

Amended 2026-08-19 after verifying the item against the code. Rationale, short
version: the unification this item was written for is already true and needs no
change, so the item is reduced to the one clause that is not satisfied.

What is already true. recording: #StateRecording has sat on #DeploySpec,
method-independent, since before the specs-to-contract rename, so StateRecording,
CaptureSet and Capture are not touched after all -- the original "touches" note
was wrong. Execute captures pre-state and post-state around executeMethod for
every method, and the bespoke second paths fell out with other decisions: the
custom deploy method in 2c5c500 (D5) and the run-path pushAndReport in 1da3a0b
(D3/D4/D6). Neither commit claimed D7; it became true as a consequence. The
no-drift-policy rule holds -- nothing compares the two digests.

What is left. ADR-016 names two stable pairing identifiers, lane_id and
target.id. e7d2efd (D10, item-0113) removed the deploy target identity without
annotating ADR-016, so the ADR still specifies a pairing surface half of which no
longer exists. Reconciling that is append-only ADR work, not a schema change,
which is why the class drops from medium to small. Item-0118 does not cover it:
that item puts ADRs out of scope by the append-only rule.

Open question for the executor: whether the kubernetes fixtures should carry
pre/post cluster-state captures to demonstrate the method-specific content D7
describes. Registry and kubernetes fixtures both declare empty capture lists
today, so nothing exercises the varying half of the property.
