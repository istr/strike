Decided by the operator on 2026-11-14 at 224ba59, while authoring
item-0016: contract/attest follows ADR-048 strictly, and the move lands
as its own item ahead of item-0016, so the direction rule item-0016 adds
holds for attest without an exception that would be written only to be
removed again.

Why the union belongs in the concept tier. The lane and the attestation
are independent constructs. They couple through the typed objects both
contain, and the only direct reference from an attestation to a lane is
the lane digest, a primitive over the raw lane file bytes. At the anchor
the one exception is lane.#Peer, named twice: in the sealed attestation's
declared peers and in the provenance's externalParameters.peers. It is a
leftover rather than a decision. Under ADR-047 it lived in base-peer.cue
as a shared declaration, and its file header still says so; 2c0fe06 moved
its two arms into contract/endpoint and kept the union in lane because
the peer decode stayed in internal/lane. It is the one hand-written union
of the contract whose arms live in another package than the union.

The type names the artifact, the field names the role (ADR-048 (1); the
same division contract/endpoint/trust.cue states for #Certificate and
#HostKey). The attestation tells declared from observed peers by its
fields peers and observedPeers, not by the type, and the container-egress
role of ADR-029 stays on the lane fields that hold peers. Registries and
the deploy target already use the arm endpoint.#TLS without that role.

Not part of this item: crossval keeps its import of lane (decided in
item-0016), and the observed-identity records keep their own attest
types.
