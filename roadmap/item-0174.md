The deferred publication-model fork of ADR-058 (Fork K''), by operator decision of 2026-10-30. State at authoring: quay.io is out under the digest-only model (a tagless manifest and its referrers were collected within a day); Gitea and GHCR are out (no referrers API, no workload-identity exchange); Docker Hub is out (realm on a second authority, ADR-058 D5); Google Artifact Registry and Azure Container Registry accept a federated token and serve the referrers API, their forty-nine-hour retention windows were running at authoring; Amazon ECR is deprioritized. The operator's leaning at authoring is the mirror model: an origin zot the operator runs, fronted by a public pull-through cache by digest, which keeps the write-side identity at one registry strike already speaks to.
