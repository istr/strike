Raised during the item-0162 analysis of 2026-10-25. #ImageRef is
`^.+@sha256:<hex>$` (contract/primitive/scalars.cue): the digest is pinned,
the locator is free. A short name such as `alpine@sha256:...` therefore
validates. For such a reference the control plane and the engine disagree
on what the locator means: go-containerregistry defaults an unqualified
name to index.docker.io/library, while c/common's NormalizeName stores a
loaded image without a registry domain under localhost/ and resolves a
short name at lookup through the engine host's unqualified-search
registries and short-name aliases. The digest keeps the content honest; the
origin and the store name diverge, and both depend on host configuration
the lane does not declare.

The four schema sites are the step image, the pack base, the capture image
and the keyless trust-root reference. The Go-side constructor is used for
lane-generated references under localhost/strike/..., which are qualified
by construction and must stay valid.

This item decides, it does not presume. Both outcomes are acceptable; what
is not acceptable is leaving the grammar as it is without a recorded
reason. Related: item-0078 and item-0082 own the typed image reference and
its constructor; item-0162 makes the control plane the fetcher for step
images, which is where the divergence becomes operational.
