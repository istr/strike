Implements ADR-056. Beta-blocking freeze event: `contract/endpoint` reaches
`contract/attest` through `lane.#Peer`, so the declared-peer shape on the
attest wire changes and the goldens rebaseline. This is the ratification the
Beta DoD amendment of 2026-09-05 anticipates.

Runs before item-0150. The bootstrap lane declares peers for the forge and the
Go module proxies and would be the first lane in the tree to put anchors on the
attest wire; authoring it against the old vocabulary means writing it twice and
touching the bootstrap Containerfile twice, once to place a bundle file and
once to remove it.

The change has no seam. Changing the schema without the fixtures leaves the
tree uncompilable; changing the fixtures without the schema leaves it
unvalidatable. Sizing at the anchor 29b6893: 53 YAML files carrying 311 trust
declarations, 45 Go files naming Fingerprint or CABundle, and the hand-written
Go types in internal/endpoint/trust.go, which are @go(-) and are not
regenerated. The bulk is mechanical fixture churn behind count gates; the risk
sits in the schema-and-codegen chain and the golden rebaseline.

Five declaration sites carry the anchor shape and must each be either migrated
or named as a deferral with an owner: lane.#OIDC.trust, the deploy registry
target's trust, endpoint.#HTTPS.trust, endpoint.#TLS.trust, and #DoT.trust.
#EngineServerTLS mirrors #Trust rather than using it; whether it follows
belongs to item-0004, which owns the observed-TLS shape.

Two questions are open at authoring time and are candidates for a
delegate-measure spike rather than prediction. Whether gopkg.in/yaml.v3 treats
a double-quoted scalar with backslash line continuation the way the YAML core
grammar does, which decides whether an anchor can be wrapped legibly at all --
measured against PyYAML the block and plain styles leave a newline or a space
and fail primitive.#Base64. And whether YAML anchors and aliases survive the
lane parser, and what the lane digest then covers, which decides whether the
four harness endpoints that share one root repeat the certificate four times.

The certificate itself is generated once and checked in: subject naming it a
test anchor, validity chosen so the fixture set has no expiry, CA:TRUE, and
self-signed over the RFC 9500 testECCP256 key. ADR-056 D8 records why this is
a boundary against ADR-018 rather than an exception to it.

## Final summary

Landed on main as ba19409 (the four halves C-a, C-b1 extraction, C-b1, C-b2 squashed). endpoint.#Certificate with mode rootca|leaf and a base64 DER body replaces #Trust, #Fingerprint, #CABundle and #TrustType at all five declaration sites; #DoT.trust unifies onto rootca; the Go types are generated, unmarshalTLSTrust and loadCABundle are deleted, leaf is a byte comparison in VerifyPeerCertificate and rootca is the sole root; mode defaults through a CUE default arm and is concrete on the attest wire. validateTrustAnchors reconciles every declared anchor at declaration level, directly after the resolver check: rootca must be CA:TRUE, self-signed and self-verifying, leaf must parse. The RFC 9500 fixture anchor is in every YAML fixture, the golden lane, the cross-validation vector and the 49 lane blocks embedded in Go tests; five new peer fixtures cover the rejection paths and two re-pointed fixtures keep the AbsPath and Digest negatives; sites that stand up a TLS server or dial the harness derive their anchor from the certificate they mint or read. Goldens reminted, seven lintdoc golden-class digests moved, make check green against the harness. Also landed: primitive.Base64.String and endpoint.CertificateFromDER. Deferred: the documentation examples (README.md, docs/DNS-RESOLVER-CONFIGURATION.md, docs/local-development.md) still show the old vocabulary and are the documentation instruction's; #EngineServerTLS stays a mirror under item-0004.
