## Final summary

Retired without implementation, superseded by ADR-051 D5. The item framed deploy-path SSH as a peer permission: lift the not-implemented guard and give deploy containers the run path's mediated SSH and per-step trust delivery. ADR-051 D5 removed the custom method and made rsync, scp, sftp and git over SSH a deploy method of its own, the transport family, with its own Layer V parts (payload from the digest pin, verified host key plus allowlisted protocol observed, step attribution, optional remote post-state capture per D7). The two methods that exist, registry and kubernetes, have no SSH use case, so lifting the guard on its own would grant a kubectl or capture container the front's git-only allowlist with no method behind it. The gate the item named was also wrong: item-0003 rehosts DoT and TLS, while the actual prerequisite is the ADR-038 D1 protocol allowlist extension (sftp subsystem, rsync --server, scp -t/-f), which no item owns. The surviving content -- per-step SSH trust delivery to a deploy container and deploy-side observed records -- belongs inside the transport-family method item when that method is wanted, with the D1 extension and item-0002's allowlisted-command record field as its blockers.
