State at authoring: quay.io is out under the digest-only model (a tagless manifest and its referrers were collected within a day); Gitea and GHCR are out (no referrers API, no workload-identity exchange); Docker Hub is out (realm on a second authority, ADR-058 D5); Google Artifact Registry and Azure Container Registry accept a federated token and serve the referrers API, their forty-nine-hour retention windows were running at authoring; Amazon ECR is deprioritized. The operator's leaning at authoring is the mirror model: an origin zot the operator runs, fronted by a public pull-through cache by digest, which keeps the write-side identity at one registry strike already speaks to. ## Amendment 2026-11-08 at cdd04f3: the redirect rule and geo-replication

By operator decision of 2026-11-05 every request carrying the declared
registry credential fails closed on any redirect (item-0173). Measured on
2026-11-05 at 060225f: GAR redirects a 32 MiB blob GET to its own host
with the bearer, ACR redirects every blob GET to a storage host without
it, and neither redirects a write, manifest, referrers or ping request.
Under the rule an authenticated read of either fails, so whether the
chosen registry needs an opt-in, and its shape, is decided here with the
publication model. Two further points for the choice: strike resolves
every dial through the run's DoT resolver, so a geo-replicated registry
can answer one run from several replicas (Azure/acr#588, "DNS bouncing");
and the deploy's read-after-write proves visibility at the replica the
producer reaches, not at a verifier's.
