Carved out of item-0166 by operator decision of 2026-10-29, in the roadmap
revision behind ADR-057, whose consequences split item-0166 into the
authenticated push and the public signing; the signing half is cut into
this item, item-0168 and item-0169.

Dependency on item-0085, by operator decision of 2026-10-29. ADR-057 states
in its Context and in D2 that oidc.identity is primitive.#StringOrURI. At
02f3c00 it is a plain string and #StringOrURI exists nowhere under
contract/; item-0085 delivers it. The discrepancy is recorded here as a
dependency, not as an erratum to the ADR. item-0081 types the same verify
and keyless chain this item extends -- the issuer and identity it reads,
the id token as the redacting secret type -- and runs ahead of this item in
_order.md as a preference, not as an edge.

Before the beta cut the builder runs on the reference endpoint under the
harness keyless chain, which stands in for the forge identity (ADR-057
D12; operator decision of 2026-10-29: pre-beta builds stay local, which
leaves time to choose the forge). The pins a forge issuer fills are
declared at the beta cut by lane edit and trust-root re-pin. Whether the
harness Fulcio can issue the extensions this item pins, given the issuer
type its configuration declares, is the delegate spike ADR-057 lists as
"the harness stand-in for the builder identity and the token lifetime";
the instruction is authored after it.

Moved here from item-0166: the keyless token is obtained at the deploy
rather than at run start, because a forge-minted token lives about five
minutes and a cold bootstrap run took about two minutes on the reference
endpoint. The token pre-check and the issued-leaf check are the same seam.

## Amendment 2026-11-02 at 2185d60: scope after the harness spike

The delegate spike of 2026-11-02 measured against the harness: Fulcio
v1.9.1 issues the pinned extensions (.11, .12, .13, .14, .18) under a URI
SAN when the realm is declared a ci-provider issuer; for that issuer type
Fulcio checks the proof of possession against sub, while strike signs the
email claim when the token carries one; a claim can be set per mint
through the Keycloak admin API; tokens of 300 and 900 seconds are
accepted.

Operator decisions of 2026-11-02. The engine version stays in the
informational statement (dated amendment to ADR-057 D10) and the
substrate-specific builder.id is item-0063's, so both leave this item's
acceptance. The keyless token is already read at the deploy, so that
clause holds as built. The harness realm becomes a ci-provider issuer and
the email issuer is removed; the client loses the email scope and the
test user stays; the harness pins sourceRepositoryRef only, and its sha
claim is a fixed value for fixtures. The extensions block is required on
the oidc block, where sourceRepositoryRef or sourceRepositoryDigest is
enforced after parse, and optional on base-SBOM signers with no minimum;
the pins are typed as primitive.GitCommit, GitRef, URIReference and
RunnerEnvironment. The builder token becomes a lane.secrets entry the
oidc block names, under the fifteen-minute bound of ADR-058 D4, as the
note of item-0081 assigns; a per-credential override is deferred to
item-0063. The item lands as two instructions: first the harness
migration together with the iss pre-check and the issued-leaf check on
SAN and issuer, then the pins and the token declaration.
