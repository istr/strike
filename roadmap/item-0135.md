Two helpers skip their own test when a listener cannot be bound:
`startTCPForwarder` in the mediator tests and the upstream forwarder in the
capsule tests, both because the case under test needs the default HTTPS port
443 and an unprivileged process cannot bind it. The skip is silent in exactly
the way item-0119 removed for the env-gated integration tests, and it hides the
default-port and bare-host branches on any machine without
CAP_NET_BIND_SERVICE. The resolution is not a wider skip message: either the
mediator's port defaulting is exercised without a real bind, or the forwarder
binds a high port and the declared peer carries it explicitly. Which of the two
applies to each site is the open question this item answers.
