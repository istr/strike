Decided with ADR-060 on 2026-11-17 and written at 36374b5. D6 has the
binary state its version; the Consequences place the command after the
freeze event of ADR-059, as a change of its own.

At 36374b5 nothing in the tree states a version: no constant, no file
and no command. The usage of cmd/strike lists run, validate, dag and
verify. The constant arrives with the freeze event, which item-0128
owns, and this item depends on it for that reason. By operator decision
of 2026-11-17 the constant is unexported in internal/deploy, and ADR-060
rejects a constant in cmd/strike that is passed into the projection, so
the command needs a way to read the value where it lives.

The command is the only way a holder of the binary learns its version.
A scanner takes the version of a Go binary from the build information,
the linker flags or the symbol table, and a compiled-in constant is in
none of them (ADR-060, Known limitations).

Open at authoring: how cmd/strike reads the constant, and where the
line goes. strike writes its own messages to standard error through the
logger; verify alone writes a result, its statements, to standard
output.

Open at ratification: whether the first release (item-0062) waits for
this item. If it does, item-0062 depends on it and it joins
beta-readiness. If it does not, it is scheduled behind every beta item
or parked.
