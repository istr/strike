Found on 2026-11-03 during the item-0166 grounding at 876fb38, read
statically and not measured: test/sigstore-local/compose.yaml publishes
the seven harness ports (8443, 5555, 3003, 3004, 6962, 5443 on Caddy, and
8853 on CoreDNS) without a host address, and podman-run(1) binds a port
published without one on every interface of the host. Caddy proxies every
Keycloak path, so the admin console of the start-dev instance with its
bootstrap admin, and the password grant of the test user, would be
reachable from the network; at the anchor the registry also accepts
anonymous pushes, which item-0166 closes for writes but not for the
reachability itself.

Parked by operator decision of 2026-11-03 as a follow-up of the item-0166
analysis, outside that item's scope. The binding is a property of the
harness topology, so item-0134 (the harness declared as a CUE topology
over libpod) is the natural vehicle if it lands first; otherwise the
compose port syntax carries it. The measurement from a non-loopback
interface address is part of the acceptance because the static reading
is not proof of the reachability.
