ADR-051 D3/D4/D6 for the registry method, merged into one sealing-point change (absorbs the former target-grammar and control-plane-push items). Structure DeployRegistry.target as endpoint.#TLS (authority plus #Trust anchor) with #OCIName, anchored to the OCI distribution-spec grammar and rejecting any tag or digest; #OCIName is a primitive; IPv6 literals stay parked in item-0101. Push the resolved artifacts image from the control plane with remote.Write against that anchor, dropping engine ImagePush and the lane-wide registry field; a redirect whose final dialed host violates the anchor fails, and only the final dialed host is attested. Generate the SBOM on the deploy path over the resolved subject, sealed before the push; remove PackSpec.packages, dead once cataloging leaves pack. build and pack become symmetric producers, no self-SBOM (ADR-043).
