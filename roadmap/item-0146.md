Discovered while landing item-0049. That change pinned the resolver's trust
anchor to endpoint.#CABundle in CUE, and the hand-written narrowing in
unmarshalDNSResolver became unreachable as a result: lane.Parse runs
schema.FillLaneJSON before json.Unmarshal, and that is the only unmarshal into
a Lane in the module, so the schema rejects a certFingerprint anchor before the
Go branch can. Measured on the landing run: all six error branches of that one
function report zero coverage.

The site is not the finding. The same question stands at unmarshalHTTPSPeer,
unmarshalTLSTrust, unmarshalKeyless, and the registry-target decode, and it
cannot be answered per site without picking a policy: a guard the schema
already rejects is a dead branch, but a decoder with no guards at all trusts
the schema absolutely and reads worse at the call site. Sibling closure is why
this is one item rather than a line edit.

Two constraints the resolution has to respect. The narrowing in
unmarshalDNSResolver cannot simply be deleted -- unmarshalTLSTrust returns the
Trust interface, so something must reduce it to the concrete field; the real
alternative is decoding the bundle directly and giving up the shared
peer-trust mechanic for that field, which changes what single-sources the wire
shape. And validateResolver's checks are a different class: they express what
CUE cannot (canonicality, ADN-is-not-an-address) and stay whatever this decides.
