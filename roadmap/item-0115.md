After item-0108 two callers -- AttachStatementBundles and the deploy SBOM
push -- apply the identical after-burner sequence to what ArtifactImage
returns: ConfigMediaType to carry the artifact type per the OCI 1.1 fallback
rule, then Subject re-applied as the outermost wrapper because any wrapper
added after Subject erases it (mutate compute() overwrites). The subject the
constructor sets internally is therefore always dead weight. Code is
liability: exactly one place should own the wrapping semantics. Two shapes to
decide at ratification or authoring time: fold the full referrer semantics
into ArtifactImage (callers pass content, artifact type, subject, optional
annotations and get a push-ready image), or strip the subject parameter and
leave wrapping entirely to callers via one shared helper. Either way the
duplication and the dead internal Subject go.
