## Amendment 2026-11-08 at cdd04f3: verify waits for required layers only

By operator decision of 2026-11-05 (item-0173), strike verify's referrer
read retries until the required layers are present and never waits for an
optional bundle. An approver bundle that a registry has not yet listed
therefore yields the grade candidate, not release; the release procedure
runs verify after the approver bundle is attached and the listing serves
it.

## Amendment 2026-11-10 at 2a52c3e: verify reads anonymously and confirms the subject is served

Operator decisions of 2026-11-09 and 2026-11-10, taken while deciding the
publication model (item-0174) and recorded in its ADR-041 and ADR-058
amendments. They were placed here on 2026-11-10 for three reasons: they
touch the same read path and the same CLI as this item, and this item
lands before item-0169, item-0062 and item-0063.

strike verify reads anonymously in UC1 and UC2, from every registry, and
holds no secret.

- The registry-token path that item-0173 landed is removed:
  --registry-token, and with it --deploy-step and --registry-ca, which said
  where the token is presented, together with their code and tests.
- docs/CLI-CONVENTIONS.md loses its private-repository block.
- The harness repository the release lane pushes to gains anonymous read
  beside its authenticated writes, as public/** already has (measured since
  item-0173). The verify before the cut therefore reads it anonymously.
- The declared credential stays for the deploy's writes, its
  read-after-write and the in-run reads of a target authority.
- A private repository cannot be verified with strike verify; this is a
  Beta DoD row 14 candidate.

strike verify also confirms that the subject manifest is served: an
anonymous HEAD of the subject inside the existing bounded wait, and a
subject that is not served is FAIL. The reason is measured on zot v2.1.22:
after a subject is collected, its referrers listing keeps answering with
the dangling referrer, so without the HEAD a pass says nothing about
whether the digest can still be pulled.

The acceptance intent above gains the anonymous-read and subject clause.
