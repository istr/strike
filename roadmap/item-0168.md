Carved out of item-0166 by operator decision of 2026-10-29, in the roadmap
revision behind ADR-057.

Required approvers in every lane (D7) touches every lane the tree carries:
bootstrap/lane.cue, the fixtures, the test lanes item-0165 holds to what
lane.Parse accepts, and the documentation snippets item-0154 gates. Before
the beta cut the approver signs with a test card that chains to the trust
service provider's test authority and is never issued by the harness
(ADR-057 D12). The test card is in transit as of 2026-10-29.

Delegate spikes ADR-057 lists that precede this item's instruction: the
verifier profile and grade logic against golden fixtures; the harness
Rekor v2 with a certificate verifier; the public TSA; the test-card
certificate (EKU, keyUsage, subject, chain, batch counter, PIN pad).

Open at authoring: ADR-057 names conformance fixtures from a test-card
bundle for contract/release. A bundle exists only once something signs
with the card, so whether that fixture lands here from a spike-assembled
bundle or with item-0169's signer is decided when this item is authored.
