Parked by ADR-057 D5, not ratified. The authorization statement is an
unmodified SLSA Verification Summary Attestation whose reproduction evidence
lives in the policy it names (the lane's reproducedBy gate and the release
procedure). A strike-specific predicate would carry that evidence
explicitly, at the cost of a new wire with no ecosystem consumer.
