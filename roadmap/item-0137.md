The two captures are structurally identical first-wins guards; whether
they consolidate into one transport-owned type (permitted by the
two-call-site rule) or stay package-local is fixed at instruction time.
The Close methods on mediator and resolver have no production caller --
capsule Stop cancels and waits but never calls either -- and only their
tests keep them alive; removal is the default resolution, and with
Close go the closed flags, the closed sentinel errors, and the Serve
pre-checks. Sequenced after item-0136: the mediator file is that item's
surface and its acceptance names the Close doc comment this item
deletes.
