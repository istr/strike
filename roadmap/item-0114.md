Owns the D9 promise item-0108 defers. item-0108 relocates SBOM generation from pack to deploy AS-IS over the single #StepImageRef subject: one image, one GenerateImageSBOM call, one record.Artifact entry fed by a single-entry adapter (run.go). The decomposition is new functionality, not a relocation -- a file/directory artifact arm beside #StepImageRef, a name-keyed artifacts map in the lane schema, per-region iteration in the cataloger, and a per-region SBOM written into each record.Artifact. Sequenced after the registry method is validated end to end (registry-first method rebuild). Depends on item-0108.
