Engine-cluster LEAD: settles the shape so the hardening matching (engine-hardening item) is written once. Golden-affecting (resolver sealed). == cross-roadmap execution-order step 8. Migrated from the retired cue-spec-review.

Amended after a whole-strand read of mediator, peer and dial: ADR-028 commits
the attestation to record "destination IP and port" per connection, but
#ObservedPeer carries only resolved, the untyped candidate list the DoT lookup
returned, and the mediator drops every address after the first without noting
which one it dialed. The observed-shape decision this item owns is the right
place to settle that field, since it touches the same block of
contract/attest/attestation.cue.

Amended 2026-08-26 at dc83c97: the fingerprint clause is removed from the
acceptance. item-0075 landed that typing in ea6e2c5 -- engine server and client
cert, the ObservedSSH host key and the ObservedTLS server cert carry
primitive.#Digest, and every construction site goes through
primitive.DigestFromHex -- so no prefixed-versus-bare-hex split is left to
unify, and contract/primitive/scalars.cue holds no bare-sha256 primitive for
the clause's second arm to select. One bare-string fingerprint remains,
#ResolverRecord.serverCertFingerprint; item-0075 descoped it deliberately,
because #Sealed.resolver is required-but-emptyable and tightening it rejects
the valid empty-resolver deploy, and item-0098 owns it together with the
resolver-block optionality. This item consolidates shapes, not formats, and
inherits one settled prefixed form.

## Amendment 2026-08-31: the shape fork is closed, pre-execution

The acceptance offered two arms -- factor one shared observed-TLS-server-identity
type, or keep the three role-distinct with the rationale recorded. The operator
ratified the first arm on 2026-08-31, before authoring. The role-distinct arm is
withdrawn and needs no rationale record.

Evidence accumulated since the item was written supports the choice. ADR-030
already names one capture mechanism -- transport.CaptureIdentity producing
transport.ConnectionIdentity -- and says the peers, the DoT resolver and the
per-peer mediator connections share it; the divergence is in the three attested
projections of that one captured value, not in the capture. item-0130 then
removed the last structural reason the projections could differ: every one of
those connections now comes from a single construction in internal/transport
that captures identity the same way for all of them, so three shapes over one
source is duplication with no remaining producer-side justification.

Boundary: #EngineServerTLS is in scope for the shared shape, but the engine's
declared-anchor and identity-capture defects belong to item-0005. This item
settles what the observed shape is; item-0005 makes the engine produce one.

## Amendment 2026-11-14 at 433e6a2: the required-collection deferral

item-0016 adds rule (6) to ADR-049 -- inside a definition, a list field
is optional or non-empty, and a map field made only of pattern
constraints is optional -- and gates it in the contract mode of
lintcue. contract/attest is frozen for the beta line, so by operator
decision of 2026-11-14 the nine attest sites of the rule are deferred
to this item, each as an entry of the lint's deferral list that names
item-0004 as owner:

- #Sealed.artifacts and #Sealed.peers (pattern maps)
- #ObservedPeer.resolved
- #Informational.provenance and #InformationalPredicate.provenance
- #SLSAProvenanceStatement.subject, #EngineContextStatement.subject and
  #InformationalStatement.subject
- #StrikeExternalParameters.peers (pattern map)

This item settles each field's form, optional or non-empty, and deletes
its deferral entry in the same change; an entry that matches no field
fails the gate, so the list cannot outlive its fields.
#ObservedPeer.resolved is already part of the observed-shape decision
above. Each change narrows or widens the frozen wire and is a freeze
event under the Beta Definition of Done, riding the golden regeneration
this item's acceptance already carries. The item is not done while an
entry of the deferral list names it.

## Amendment 2026-11-20 at 5490287: what item-0198 leaves to this item

item-0198 generalizes rule (6) of ADR-049 by a dated amendment: no field
holds "", null, [] or {} as written, no struct-valued field accepts {}, a
field with a default is a regular field with an explicit default arm, and
every field typed with a discriminator vocabulary carries the required
marker. Its evaluation lint replaces the syntactic required-collection
check, and with it the deferral list of the amendment of 2026-11-14 above.
Measured at 5490287 by a prototype of that lint, contract/attest holds 43
sites; by operator decision each is a deferral naming item-0004 as owner,
the nine collection sites above among them. This item settles every one
of them in its freeze event and deletes the entry in the same change.

Decided for the attest sites:

- The four pattern maps whose entries are lists, the declared peers and
  the peer attribution, twice each, hold a non-empty list per entry
  (`[K]: [T, ...T]`). collectObservedPeers folds the peers of the
  captures into the declared peers of a step. Read at 39cd1d1 and not
  measured, it creates the key of a deploy step whose captures declare no
  peers with a nil list, which serializes as null. A test of a successful
  deploy with a capture without peers settles it, and the fold creates no
  key without a peer.
- #Attestation.engineDependent becomes optional (ratified 2026-11-18).
- The discriminators of the attested identities (#ObservedSSH.type,
  #ObservedTLS.type) carry the required marker.
- The definitions of contract/endpoint that contract/attest imports,
  #EngineUnix among them, change in item-0198 as a freeze event of their
  own, not here. This supersedes the decision of 2026-11-20 that gave
  #EngineUnix to this item.

The item is not done while an entry of the deferral list names it.
