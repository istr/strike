Register row R-24, moved here from item-0081 on 2026-11-01.

Measured at b4752b7: four vocabularies named the same three-way split. The
trust layer in contract/trustlayers was V | E | informational; the
dev.strike.statement annotation, a Go-only literal set, was sealed |
engine-context | informational; the frozen #Attestation sections are sealed |
engineDependent | informational; #PublishedIn names the predicates,
slsa-provenance | engine-context | informational | bundle | none. The first
three name a trust property and collapse onto one vocabulary; the fourth
names the published predicate and stays, because its names match the
predicateType URIs the attest wire freeze holds.

Decisions of 2026-11-01. The vocabulary is sealed | engine-dependent |
informational: all three are properties of what a verifier must trust,
engine-context names content rather than a property, and engine-dependent
already matches the frozen collect-model section, so the attest freeze is
not touched. The definition is named #TrustLayer. It lives in
contract/record, generated into internal/record: go-arch-lint lets the
services tier (internal/registry) import concept-tier packages but not
internal/deploy, where contract/attest generates, and contract/primitive
holds irreducible scalars only. The annotation value changes from
engine-context to engine-dependent; nothing is published yet, since the
first public publication is the beta cut. Producers and consumers are typed
in this item rather than in item-0081, so each literal is touched once.
