Raised by the operator on 2026-11-14 at b1d34df, while classifying the
list fields of item-0016.

The Beta Definition of Done amendment of 2026-11-14 fixes the frozen
baseline as the tree of contract/attest: a verifier compares
git rev-parse <commit>:contract/attest against the commit that carries
the amendment. The attest wire is defined by more than that directory.
contract/attest imports endpoint, primitive, provenance and record, and
its declared peers are endpoint.#Endpoint. item-0016 makes
endpoint.#SSH.knownHosts a non-empty list. That narrows what the attest
wire accepts while the tree of contract/attest stays the same, so the
comparison cannot see it; item-0016 records the narrowing as a freeze
event in a dated amendment by hand, the way 24d4fee was recorded.

The reverse also holds: a comment-only change inside contract/attest
moves its tree and the baseline with it, although the wire stays the
same, as the amendment of 2026-11-14 notes for the comments it changed.
