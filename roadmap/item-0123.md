Defect against an accepted ADR, found by the adversarial audit of the registry-deploy sealing path at 41c44c4. ADR-051 decides the subject three times: D4 says the pushed registry digest is the signed subject and the signature covers the artifact as it exists in the registry; D6 says the digest is the result of the push, bound afterward in the attestation; D10 claims a registry deploy carries the resulting pushed digest. The implementation seals the produced digest instead: the pushed digest exists exactly once, as the referrer attach subject, and reaches no attestation field. The two digests provably differ on every run because podman re-compresses layer blobs on export (measured: deterministic and reproducible per engine version, but a different gzip stream than the controller wrote), so the sealed record points at an identity the registry never serves and the contract comment on Sealed.artifacts (consumer-dereferenceable from the registry, C3) does not hold. Second finding: the export read is the only unverified handoff on the sealing path. The load side is digest-checked fail-closed; the export side has no counterpart, so under a distrusted engine the pushed bytes are engine-supplied unchecked, against the ADR-051 principle that what is attested is what is delivered, by construction. The available check is the measured invariant pair: the config blob digest and the layer diff_ids survive the round trip untouched, so the deploy path can verify the exported image against the produced content identity before signing. Two consequential findings fall out of the fix: the push-path comment claims the digest divergence is recorded, not reacted to, per ADR-016, but it is a log line, not a record -- with both digests in the attestation it becomes a true record; and the published evidence set is currently split, referrers and SBOM subject binding to the pushed digest while the sealed statement inside those referrers names the produced one. Reserved for ratification and instruction time, not decided here: where the produced content identity comes from at deploy time (carried forward in the output handle versus re-derived from the engine store manifest under the produced digest, the latter being another engine read that must not become the check trusting its own subject); and the schema seam for the pushed digest given the item-0114 D9 reshape of the artifacts map (an own sealed field for the one pushed subject versus a field inside record.Artifact), which is why this item links item-0114 and should land before it -- the defect concerns the already-landed registry path, D9 is new functionality on top. Neighbors that touch the same territory without owning this: item-0055 pins the sealed field set in the conformance test and gains the new field; item-0098 edits the same attestation.cue with its own golden regeneration; item-0122 rewrites the same push-path files for host-storage reasons; item-0004 types the push connection fingerprint. Every CUE change in here is confirmation-gated per AGENTS.md; the byte-exact shape is authored at execution against the then-current pin.
