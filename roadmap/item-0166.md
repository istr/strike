Split out of item-0150 by operator decision of 2026-10-26: item-0150 is the workstation proof under the local harness, the only target that needs no new capability. At 6a49956 the registry push carries no credentials (remote.Write without an authenticator; #DeployRegistryTarget has no credential field), and its transport admits only the declared authority, so a token service on another host fails even with credentials. item-0150 raises the harness token lifetime to 600 seconds for the workstation proof; a forge cannot be configured that way. Sigstore announced Rekor v2 GA on 2025-10-10 with the public instance log2025-1 to be replaced by a 2026 instance and advised against hard-coding its URL; strike declares keyless endpoints statically in the lane.

## Amendment 2026-10-29 at 02f3c00: narrowed to the authenticated push under ADR-057

ADR-057 splits this item into the authenticated push and the public
signing. By operator decision of 2026-10-29 this item keeps its id and
becomes the authenticated push; the signing half is cut three ways, along
the record's three surfaces: item-0167 (the builder identity, D2),
item-0168 (approvers, the release statement contract and the graded
verdict, D3, D5, D7) and item-0169 (the signer and the release procedure,
D4, D6, D8, D9, D11). item-0062 depends on all four.

The clauses of the acceptance above that are not the push move with the
split. The keyless token obtained at the deploy goes to item-0167, which
owns the producer's identity seam. The public chain's endpoints and trust
material, and the rotation of the public Rekor v2 instance, go to
item-0063: by operator decision of 2026-10-29 pre-beta builds stay on the
reference endpoint under the harness (ADR-057 D12), and the public chain
first serves the forge build. The browser-free public-chain token is no
longer a measurement: ADR-057 makes the builder a forge workload identity
and admits no browser flow, and item-0008 is retired as superseded. The
three-bundle verification with strike verify and cosign is the release's,
under ADR-057 D7 and D11, and rides item-0062.

The push target is the final repository of ADR-057 D6, private before the
beta cut and public after it; nothing is moved or re-pushed between the
two. Standing operator decisions of 2026-10-27 for the registry choice:
Codeberg is excluded by its terms of service; quay.io stays a candidate;
the spike evaluates at least two candidates.
