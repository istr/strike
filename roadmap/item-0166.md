## Amendment 2026-10-29 at 02f3c00: narrowed to the authenticated push under ADR-057

signing. By operator decision of 2026-10-29 this item keeps its id and
becomes the authenticated push; the signing half is cut three ways, along
the record's three surfaces: item-0167 (the builder identity, D2),
item-0168 (approvers, the release statement contract and the graded
verdict, D3, D5, D7) and item-0169 (the signer and the release procedure,

owns the producer's identity seam. The public chain's endpoints and trust
material, and the rotation of the public Rekor v2 instance, go to
item-0063: by operator decision of 2026-10-29 pre-beta builds stay on the

first serves the forge build. The browser-free public-chain token is no

three-bundle verification with strike verify and cosign is the release's,

The push target is the final repository of ADR-057 D6, private before the

## Amendment 2026-10-29 at 84b5753: the credential is a workload-identity token, the targets are zot and quay.io, the item runs after item-0167

Operator decisions of 2026-10-29, after a grounding of the tree and a
primary-source survey of zot, Gitea, GHCR and quay.io. The mechanism is
the one ADR-025 deferred as "OIDC workload identity for non-SSH client
authentication (HTTPS peers, OCI registries)" and that ADR-037 D5.1,
ADR-038 D3 and ADR-054 D5 left open: a new record, ADR-058, settles it
before this item is authored, fork by fork as ADR-057 was. The decisions
already taken: the credential is an OIDC identity token minted with the
registry's own audience, never a static password or a personal access
token ("PAT is dead, at least for strike"); where the registry validates
the token itself the token is the bearer (zot `bearer.oidc`), where the
registry federates robots the token is exchanged on the registry's own
host for a one-hour robot token and the robot name is declared in clear
(quay.io, `/oauth2/federation/robot/token`); the declaration carries the
secret reference apart from the target's trust anchor as before.

Gitea and GHCR are struck as candidates: neither offers a workload-identity
exchange for its registry (Gitea's job token cannot push packages, GHCR
has nothing outside its own workflows), and neither serves the OCI 1.1
referrers API that strike's verification discovers bundles through.
quay.io is the public candidate: private repositories are a paid plan,
accepted; the risk that a tagless manifest is garbage-collected after its
temporary push tag expires is open and is the first measurement of the
spike, on a free public repository, before any paid plan starts. The
harness stands in for the registry identity as it does for the builder
identity (ADR-057 D12): zot is switched to `bearer.oidc`, and the harness
Keycloak issues the registry-audience token, both in scope of this item.
Whether one token carries two audiences or two tokens are minted, the
realm-on-another-host case, the ambient keychain on the write path and
the read side of strike verify against a private repository are ADR-058
forks, not decided here.

Order, ratified 2026-10-29: item-0085, item-0081, item-0167, then this
item. item-0167 turns the token read at the deploy into a source that
mints per audience, which this item uses for the registry audience
instead of building a parallel channel; item-0081 puts the identity token
on the redacting secret type, which the second token inherits; item-0167's
harness spike configures the same Keycloak this item extends. Both are
therefore dependencies of this item, not ordering preferences.

## Amendment 2026-10-31 at 26d13fd: ADR-058 is the record; the pre-beta push goes to the harness zot

ADR-058 landed at 26d13fd and settles every mechanism fork the amendment
above left open: the credential is a reference into lane.secrets carried
in a credential union beside the trust anchor (D1, D2); two tokens, one
per audience (D3); the lifetime bound of fifteen minutes or the issuer's
shortest supported lifetime, enforced before use (D4); one authority, a
realm elsewhere unsupported (D5); the ambient keychain removed (D6); the
environment clause of ADR-006 read for the property it protects (D7); the
record carries no client identity (D8). The acceptance above is restated
against that record.

The target changes. quay.io is no longer a candidate: the delegate spike
of 2026-10-29 measured that a tagless, subject-referenced manifest and its
referrers are collected within a day of the temporary push tag's expiry,
which contradicts the digest-only model of ADR-057 D6; the robot
federation convention it introduced stays in the union for operators who
run a Quay with retention they control, and this item verifies that arm
against a hermetic stand-in answering the measured endpoint shapes. The
pre-beta push is against the harness zot under bearer.oidc (ADR-058 D9,
ADR-057 D12 extended to the pusher role); the first public push is the
forge build's, and its target is decided by item-0174 before the release
cut. No paid registry plan is started for this item.

Two pieces of work leave this item: the explicit manifest artifactType and
the refusal of the referrers tag fallback go to item-0172, which runs
first and is now a dependency; the read side of strike verify under the
lane's anchor and credential goes to item-0173, which follows. Parked
successors recorded by ADR-058: the declared realm peer (item-0175), the
spiffe:// source (item-0176), KMS-held client identity and agent identity
filtering from ADR-025's deferral list (item-0177, item-0178).
