Split out of item-0150 by operator decision of 2026-10-26: item-0150 is the workstation proof under the local harness, the only target that needs no new capability. At 6a49956 the registry push carries no credentials (remote.Write without an authenticator; #DeployRegistryTarget has no credential field), and its transport admits only the declared authority, so a token service on another host fails even with credentials. item-0150 raises the harness token lifetime to 600 seconds for the workstation proof; a forge cannot be configured that way. Sigstore announced Rekor v2 GA on 2025-10-10 with the public instance log2025-1 to be replaced by a 2026 instance and advised against hard-coding its URL; strike declares keyless endpoints statically in the lane.
